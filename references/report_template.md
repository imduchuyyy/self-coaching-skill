Keep the whole thing under ~1 page of terminal output. Write it in the user's
language. Replace every bracketed part with what you actually observed.

---

**AI usage review — [N] sessions, [date range]**
[X] quick tasks / [Y] deep tasks. Groups 1–4 scored on the deep tasks.

| Metric family | Score | What the history shows |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Chất lượng đầu vào | [n]/5 | [count-based observation, e.g. "12 of 21 deep tasks opened with under 15 words; 3 stated a goal"] |
| 2. Tương tác & lặp | [n]/5 | [e.g. "median 3 human turns; 4 sessions ended after the first answer; 5 real challenges"] |
| 3. Xác minh | [n]/5 | [e.g. "tests run in 18 sessions; the AI was corrected in 3; no source ever requested"] |
